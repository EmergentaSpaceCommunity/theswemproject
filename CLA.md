## Why this agreement exists

SWEM is published under the GNU Affero General Public License, version 3 or later. The copyright
holder also offers commercial licences to people who cannot accept the AGPL's obligations.

Offering a commercial licence requires holding enough rights over every part of the work to grant
it. Without an agreement like this one, each contributor keeps the copyright in what they wrote, and
a commercial licence for the whole could not be granted without asking every contributor
individually, forever. This agreement asks you for the rights that make that possible.

It does **not** ask you to give up your copyright. You keep it, and you may use and license your own
contribution however else you wish.

## Notes for the review, not part of the agreement

- **Individual vs. entity.** A version for corporate contributors is usually a separate document
  naming authorised employees. This draft covers only individuals; whether the project needs the
  corporate form depends on who is expected to contribute.
- **Alternative considered and set aside.** A Developer Certificate of Origin (DCO) sign-off is much
  lighter on contributors and is what many AGPL projects use. It was set aside here because a DCO
  certifies origin without granting the copyright holder any right to relicense, and the commercial
  offer in `LICENSES.md` depends on holding that right. If the commercial offer is ever withdrawn, a
  DCO becomes the better choice and this document should be replaced by one.
- **Scope.** This agreement covers every contribution to this repository, all of which is
  AGPL-3.0-or-later.
- **Where the burden lands.** Requiring a CLA does reduce drive-by contributions. That cost is
  accepted deliberately here, in exchange for the commercial offer staying possible.
