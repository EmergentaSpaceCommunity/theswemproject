# Contributing

Issues, ideas and patches are welcome.

Before a patch can be merged its author agrees to the Contributor License Agreement in `CLA.md`.
You keep the copyright in what you wrote; the agreement lets the copyright holder license it
together with the rest of SWEM, under the AGPL and under other terms. `LICENSES.md` says why.

The agreement is a draft and is not in force yet. Until it is adopted, a patch from outside is
read and discussed and is not merged.

What to run before sending a change is in `AGENTS.md`, under "Definition of done".
